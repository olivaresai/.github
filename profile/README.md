**Run, connect and govern the AI that works for you, on your own infrastructure.**

</div>

Olivares is the base layer for AI work, the way Linux and systemd are for an operating system. The tools stay what they are: Claude Code, Codex, Grok, OpenCode, Ollama and the models behind them. Olivares runs them as governed sessions and connects them to your folders, repositories, data, secrets, people and systems.

- **Sessions you can trust.** Each session runs confined to its folder. Approvals, policies and a hash-chained audit trail record who did what.
- **Your accounts, your models.** Sign each tool in once for your organization, or run local models with Ollama. Your governance data stays on your servers.
- **One binary.** A single self-hosted Go binary with the console built in. SQLite to start, PostgreSQL for teams. Linux and macOS.
- **We do not break users.** What a release publishes stays compatible; a removal comes with a deprecation first and an automatic migration.
